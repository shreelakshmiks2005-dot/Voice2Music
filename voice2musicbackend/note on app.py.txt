You can download FFmpeg for Windows easily. Here's a step-by-step:

Step 1: Go to FFmpeg official site

URL: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

Click Windows → You'll be redirected to the Windows builds by BtbN or Gyan.dev.

Step 2: Download a build

On Gyan.dev, choose Release builds → ffmpeg-release-essentials.zip (smallest, stable build).

On BtbN, choose ffmpeg-release-full.7z or zip.
